## Our Process

### 1. Consultation

We review the project requirements, design preferences, dimensions, budget, and material options.

### 2. Material Selection

The appropriate marble, granite, quartz, or other stone surface is selected based on style, durability, and application.

### 3. Measurement

Accurate dimensions are taken before fabrication.

### 4. Fabrication

Stone is professionally cut, shaped, polished, and finished according to project specifications.

### 5. Quality Inspection

Each piece is reviewed for accuracy, finish, appearance, and overall quality.

### 6. Installation

The completed stone elements are professionally installed.

### 7. Final Review

The project is inspected to ensure proper fit, finish, alignment, and presentation.
